Privacy Policy for Talking Clock & Alarm

Last Updated: February 7, 2025

Introduction

Talking Clock & Alarm ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Our app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Alarm Settings: Alarm times, repeat schedules, custom messages, and alarm preferences
  • Timer Settings: Productivity timer configurations (work duration, break duration, cycles)
  • Health Reminders: Water reminder intervals, medication (shown as Supplements in the app) schedules, yoga timer settings
  • Talking Clock Settings: Voice announcement preferences and quiet hours (uses device volume)
  • Daily Routines: Routine tasks and schedules
  • App Preferences: User interface preferences and settings

Important: All data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (Room database and SharedPreferences). We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers.

No Personal Information Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Payment information
  • Any other personally identifiable information

How We Use Information

The information stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Provide alarm and timer functionality
  • Deliver health reminders (water, medication (shown as Supplements in the app), yoga)
  • Enable voice time announcements (Talking Clock feature)
  • Maintain your app preferences and settings
  • Restore alarms and reminders after device restart

Data Storage and Security

  • Storage Location: All data is stored locally on your device
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not use cloud storage or backup services
  • No Data Transmission: No data is transmitted over the internet
  • Data Security: Your data is protected by Android's built-in security mechanisms
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device. If you have Android backup enabled (e.g. to your Google account), the system may have backed up this app's data; if you reinstall the app, Android may restore that backup, so your alarms, timers, and reminders can reappear. To avoid restore on reinstall, you can turn off backup for this app or clear app data before uninstalling.

Permissions We Request

Our app requests the following permissions to provide core functionality:

Required Permissions

  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps your device awake to ensure alarms and timers function reliably
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Restores your alarms and reminders after device restart
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Allows precise alarm scheduling for accurate timing
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays notifications for alarms, timers, and reminders
  • VIBRATE: Provides vibration feedback for alarms and notifications
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Runs background services for alarms and time announcements
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used for alarm sound and talking clock voice playback
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Used for the talking clock time-announcement service (declared with a user-visible justification)
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Displays full-screen alarm interface when device is locked

How Permissions Are Used

  • Permissions are used exclusively for app functionality
  • No permission is used for data collection or tracking
  • All permissions are necessary for the app's core features

Third-Party Services

Google Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Our app uses the Google Text-to-Speech service (a system service provided by Android) to enable voice announcements. This service:

  • Is provided by Google and subject to Google's privacy policy
  • Processes text-to-speech conversion locally on your device
  • Does not transmit your alarm messages or settings to Google
  • Only uses the TTS engine for voice synthesis

Gson Library

We use the Gson library for local JSON parsing only. This library does not transmit any data or connect to external services.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. All information remains on your device and is never sent to external servers or services.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is stored locally on your device and accessible through the app
  • Deletion: Delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device settings. Note: if Android backup was enabled, reinstalling may restore that data (see Data Backup and Data Storage and Security above).
  • Modification: Modify or delete individual alarms, timers, and reminders through the app interface
  • Opt-Out: Since we don't collect data, there's nothing to opt out of

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, the app is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Data Backup

Android's built-in backup system may include this app's data (alarms, timers, reminders, settings) in device backups. This backup is:

  • Stored on your Google account (if backup is enabled)
  • Subject to Google's privacy policy
  • Not accessible by us
  • Can be disabled in your device settings

If you reinstall the app: When backup is enabled, Android may restore the backed-up data after reinstall, so your previously set alarms, timers, and reminders may appear again. This is done by the system, not by us. To start with a clean slate after reinstall, disable backup for this app before uninstalling, or clear the app's data before uninstalling.

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app or on our website (if applicable)

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
